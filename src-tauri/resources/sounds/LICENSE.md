# Sound cue provenance

Every `.wav` under `default/` was **synthesised for this repository** by
`tools/gen-sounds.py`. They are not samples, not recordings, and not derived
from any existing work: each one is a sum of sine partials with an exponential
envelope, written directly to PCM.

They are therefore original to this project and shipped under the repository's
MIT licence, with no third-party terms attached.

This file exists because the alternative is worse. A bundled sound with unclear
provenance is a shipping problem, not a nicety — it is the kind of thing nobody
checks until an app is being distributed, at which point the person who has to
answer "where did this come from" is not the person who added it. Regenerating
is one command and the script is in-tree, so there is never a reason for an
asset here to have an unknown origin.

**If you add a sound that is not generated by that script, record its source and
licence here, in this file, in the same commit.** CC0 or an equivalent public
dedication only.

## The cues

| File | Cue | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `turn-finished.wav` | `TurnFinished` | Rising major third (E5→G♯5). Resolved, quiet, unremarkable — it plays often |
| `turn-failed.wav` | `TurnFailed` | Falling minor second (G♯4→G4). Unresolved on purpose: distinguishable from success without being louder than it |
| `attention.wav` | `Attention` | One note (D5). The most-played cue, so the least eventful |
| `conflict.wav` | `Conflict` | Two low notes a tritone apart (D4, G♯4). The one cue allowed to sound uneasy |
| `run-adopted.wav` | `RunAdopted` | Major arpeggio (C5–E5–G5). Adopting a fan-out is the one moment here worth a small fanfare |

All are mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, and under a second. Mono because a notification
cue that pans is a notification cue that sounds broken on one headphone.

## The `silent` pack

Not a directory of silent files — an empty map. `sound::play` on a pack with no
entry for a cue returns without touching the audio device, so the silent pack
costs nothing at runtime and cannot drift out of sync with the cue list when a
cue is added.
